About the Laboratory

Molecular Plant Virology Laboratory

Research-driven work in plant viruses, phytoplasmas and molecular plant pathology.

The Molecular Plant Virology Laboratory is part of the Department of Microbiology at Barkatullah Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal.

The laboratory works on molecular and genetic studies of plant pathogens, with particular emphasis on plant viruses, begomoviruses, phytoplasmas and associated disease complexes.

Research activities involve molecular identification, characterization, genetic diversity, evolutionary analysis and the study of emerging plant diseases affecting important crops and plants.

The laboratory also supports postgraduate and doctoral research and encourages collaborative scientific work in plant microbiology and molecular plant pathology.

Research Focus

  • Molecular Plant Virology
  • Begomovirus identification and characterization
  • Plant-virus genetic diversity
  • Phytoplasma-associated diseases
  • Molecular plant pathology
  • Plant pathogen evolution

Research Areas

Major scientific themes explored by the laboratory.

🔬

Molecular Plant Virology

Molecular investigation of plant viruses and virus-associated diseases using molecular and genetic approaches.

Genetic Diversity

Analysis of genetic diversity, molecular variation and evolutionary relationships among plant pathogens.

Begomoviruses

Identification and characterization of begomoviruses and associated satellite molecules infecting economically important plants.

Phytoplasmas

Crop Diseases
